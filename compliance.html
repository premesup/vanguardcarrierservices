Common questions

Compliance — frequently asked

What happens if I miss an IFTA deadline?
Late IFTA filings can mean penalties and interest — we track due dates well ahead of the deadline so filings go in on time.
Do you handle permits for oversize loads?
Yes, state-specific oversize and overweight permits are part of the compliance service.
Can you help if I already have a DOT audit scheduled?
Yes — we can review and organize your files ahead of a scheduled audit, though earlier notice gives us more time to prepare.
Do you replace my insurance broker?
No, we track renewal deadlines and paperwork, but your insurance broker remains your carrier for coverage itself.

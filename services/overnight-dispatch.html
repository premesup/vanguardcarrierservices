Common questions

Overnight dispatch — common questions

Can you cover only nights and weekends?
Yes. Carriers can arrange overnight-only, weekend, after-hours or full 24/7 dispatch support.
Do we have to outsource daytime dispatch too?
No. Vanguard can take over driver communications and load monitoring after your daytime team signs off.
Do you find loads or negotiate rates?
No. Your carrier finds and books freight, sets rates and retains its customer relationships. We coordinate loads already in your operation.
What happens when a truck breaks down at night?
We can help coordinate outside towing and repair providers, communicate updates and keep the appropriate parties informed. We do not perform repairs.
Do you work with small fleets and established carriers?
Yes. We support owner-operators, growing trucking companies and established fleets nationwide.

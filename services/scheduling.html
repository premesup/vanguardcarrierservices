Common questions

Scheduling — frequently asked

How does this work with my dispatcher?
Scheduling and dispatch coordinate directly — your dispatcher books the load, scheduling locks in the appointment windows around it.
Can you help with detention pay claims?
Yes, we track dock arrival and departure times, which is the documentation most brokers require for a detention claim.
What if a shipper won't confirm a window in advance?
We stay on it and update your driver as soon as a window is confirmed, so there's no guessing en route.
Does this cover multi-stop loads?
Yes, sequencing and timing for multi-stop loads is part of the service.

Common questions

Dispatch — frequently asked

Do I lose control of my truck's schedule?
No. Your carrier chooses its freight, customers and rates. Vanguard coordinates driver communications and execution under your operating procedures.
What hours is dispatch actually staffed?
24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Overnight and weekend loads get the same live attention as a Tuesday afternoon.
Do you dispatch carriers in every state?
Yes — dispatch coverage is nationwide across the lower 48, on major interstates and regional lanes alike, not limited to any one region.
Can you support loads we book with our own customers?
Yes. You continue booking your own freight and working with your existing customers. We manage dispatch communications and shipment follow-through as agreed.
Do you find loads or negotiate freight rates?
No. Vanguard is an outsourced carrier operations and dispatch support team. We do not source freight, book loads or negotiate rates.

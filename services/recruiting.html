Common questions

Recruiting — frequently asked

Do I still make the final hiring decision?
Yes. We source, screen, and present qualified candidates — the final call on who drives for you is always yours.
How fast can you fill an open seat?
It depends on lane and equipment type, but most carriers see qualified candidates within one to two weeks of an open req.
Do you handle owner-operator recruiting too?
Yes, both company driver and owner-operator recruiting are covered.
What screening do you run before presenting a candidate?
MVR, PSP records, and employment verification at minimum, with additional checks available depending on your requirements.

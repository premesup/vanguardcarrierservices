Common questions

Breakdown Desk — frequently asked

Do you pay for the repair?
No — Vanguard coordinates the repair and vendor relationship, but repair and towing costs are billed to you or your insurance as usual. We handle the logistics, not the invoice.
What if the breakdown happens at 2 a.m.?
The breakdown desk is staffed 24/7. Middle-of-the-night calls get the same response as a weekday afternoon.
Do you have vendor coverage in every state?
Yes — our vendor network spans the lower 48, so wherever the truck goes down, we're working from an existing list of shops and tow providers, not a cold search.
Can you work with my preferred repair shops?
Yes. Tell us your preferred vendors and we'll use them first whenever the truck is in range.
What information do you need from the driver?
Location, truck and trailer number, and a description of the problem. We take it from there.
